Chart 6: State Growth in Online Shopping

Regional states such as Queensland and Western Australia experienced the strongest growth in online shopping activity, while highly urbanised states including New South Wales and Victoria recorded negative year-on-year growth. This suggests that consumer behaviour after the pandemic shifted differently across Australia, with regional consumers continuing to rely heavily on eCommerce while metro areas returned more strongly to physical retail shopping.

Chart 7: Shopper Segments and Spending Contribution

Although Super Shoppers represent only 16% of Australian online shoppers, they account for half of all online spending volume. In contrast, Sometime Shoppers make up the largest share of shoppers (30%) but contribute only 4% of total spending. This highlights the importance of retaining high-frequency online shoppers, as a relatively small customer segment generates a disproportionate share of eCommerce revenue.
